# 1.22.0 — Browser pop-outs, audio mixer, and manual stream sync

**Scope clarification:** This release does not add multiple native PiP windows in Chrome. Chrome allows one native PiP video across tabs. Multi-view provides ordinary browser pop-outs, without always-on-top behavior.

- Open several existing player tabs in independent, resizable pop-out windows.
- Balance source-player volume, mute/unmute, mute listed players, and play/pause from one mixer.
- Rewind a faster feed by 1–600 seconds where its available DVR range permits it; fine tune with −1 / +1 second.
- Use a 1–600-second timed hold when seeking is unavailable. Resume is requested in the source page, even after the mixer closes. Cancel leaves the player paused.
- Preserve tabs and playback sessions on pop-out/return, and restore them to a new normal window when the original window has closed.
- Reject stale document/video/source controls and excluded sites. Pause automatic PiP, ad comfort and smart speed for popped-out tabs without changing saved settings.

Open through the toolbar context menu: **Multi-view & audio mixer → Open multi-view & audio mixer**. Or assign the new mixer/pop-out commands in Chrome's shortcut settings. Reload the extension and video tabs after updating.

Pop-outs are browser windows, not always-on-top PiP. Source services retain control over buffering, seeking, autoplay, DRM and account stream limits. A timed hold cannot manufacture a DVR window, and some services resume at live. Different broadcasts still require manual alignment and occasional correction. Paid streaming providers have not been certified by the local test fixtures.
