Sync RedZone with a slower game feed

  1. Start both streams, then refresh players and pop out each one.
  2. On the faster feed, rewind by the estimated gap. Use −1 / +1 second to line up a shared play or scoreboard change.
  3. If rewind is unavailable, try a timed hold. It pauses now and requests resume after the chosen time, even if you close this mixer.

Available rewind is the source player’s seek range, not measured broadcast latency. A hold does not record video or create a DVR buffer. Services may discard paused content, jump to live, or block resume. Different broadcasts can drift; sync is manual.

Pop-outs suspend automatic PiP, ad comfort and smart speed. For streams left in normal tabs, disable speed-changing features in settings while syncing. Video view is optional and requires a top-page player; turn it off if the site’s controls stop working.
